About

The Story Behind the Work

Animals have always been at the centre of my life — not just as companions, but as a vocation I have been working hard to turn into a career.

I recently completed the Pet Care & Veterinary Practice Assistant diploma through PetSense College, where I developed a solid foundation in animal health, welfare, and veterinary support. This formal qualification adds structure to years of hands-on experience: almost four years actively training and competing in dog obedience and agility, 1.5 years volunteering at a Pitbull rescue organisation, and a lifelong involvement with horses — both as a rider and a caretaker.

Alongside my animal care journey, I spent 14 years as a certified FIH Level 1 hockey umpire and five years as a paid part-time hockey coach at Wagpos High School — roles that developed strong skills in leadership, youth mentorship, and composure under pressure.

Originally from South Africa, I relocated to the Netherlands in September 2023 and most recently to Berlin in March 2025. I hold a Dutch B1 certificate and am actively learning German.
